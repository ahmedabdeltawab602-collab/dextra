STATISTICS FUNDAMENTALS - FORMULA QUICK REFERENCE
============================================================

[F-M02-L04-01      ]  Classical probability                        
                    Lesson: M02-L04
                    Expr:   P = Favorable / Possible
                    Use:    Symmetric setups: coins, dice, lottery.

[F-M02-L04-02      ]  Frequentist probability                      
                    Lesson: M02-L04
                    Expr:   P = Favorable observed / Total observations
                    Use:    Repeated experiments, large samples.

[F-M02-L04-03      ]  Bayes' theorem                               
                    Lesson: M02-L04
                    Expr:   P(A | B) = P(B | A) · P(A) / P(B)
                    Use:    Have prior knowledge + new evidence.

[F-M03-L02-01      ]  Population mean                              
                    Lesson: M03-L02
                    Expr:   μ = (Σ xᵢ) / N
                    Use:    Have data for entire population.

[F-M03-L02-02      ]  Sample mean                                  
                    Lesson: M03-L02
                    Expr:   x̄ = (Σ xᵢ) / n
                    Use:    Working with a sample (most real cases).

[F-M03-L03-01      ]  Range                                        
                    Lesson: M03-L03
                    Expr:   Range = Max − Min
                    Use:    Quick first look; small data.

[F-M03-L03-02      ]  IQR                                          
                    Lesson: M03-L03
                    Expr:   IQR = Q3 − Q1
                    Use:    Skewed data or outliers present.

[F-M03-L03-03      ]  Outlier fence                                
                    Lesson: M03-L03
                    Expr:   Lower: Q1 − 1.5·IQR  ·  Upper: Q3 + 1.5·IQR
                    Use:    Identifying outliers in skewed data.

[F-M03-L04-01      ]  Population variance                          
                    Lesson: M03-L04
                    Expr:   σ² = Σ(xᵢ − μ)² / N
                    Use:    Whole population available.

[F-M03-L04-02      ]  Sample variance                              
                    Lesson: M03-L04
                    Expr:   s² = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n − 1)
                    Use:    Working with a sample (most cases).

[F-M03-L04-03      ]  Standard deviation                           
                    Lesson: M03-L04
                    Expr:   σ = √σ²    or    s = √s²
                    Use:    Always - it's the headline measure.

[F-M03-L05-01      ]  Empirical rule                               
                    Lesson: M03-L05
                    Expr:   μ ± 1σ ≈ 68%  ·  μ ± 2σ ≈ 95%  ·  μ ± 3σ ≈ 99.7%
                    Use:    Data is approximately Normal.

[F-M03-L05-02      ]  Z-score                                      
                    Lesson: M03-L05
                    Expr:   Z = (x − μ) / σ
                    Use:    Comparing values across different distributions.

[F-M03-L06-01      ]  Excess kurtosis                              
                    Lesson: M03-L06
                    Expr:   Excess kurtosis = Kurtosis − 3
                    Use:    Comparing tail risk across distributions.

[F-M03-L07-01      ]  Pearson skewness                             
                    Lesson: M03-L07
                    Expr:   Skew ≈ 3·(Mean − Median)/σ
                    Use:    Quick check on skew direction and strength.

[F-M03-L10-01      ]  Pearson correlation r                        
                    Lesson: M03-L10
                    Expr:   r = Σ(xᵢ−x̄)(yᵢ−ȳ) / [(n−1)·sₓ·s_y]
                    Use:    Measuring linear association between two numeric variables.

[F-M03-L11-01      ]  Regression line                              
                    Lesson: M03-L11
                    Expr:   Y = m·X + b
                    Use:    Predicting a numeric outcome from one numeric predictor.

[F-M03-L11-02      ]  Slope                                        
                    Lesson: M03-L11
                    Expr:   m = r · (s_y / sₓ)
                    Use:    Computing slope from correlation and std devs.

[F-M03-L11-03      ]  Intercept                                    
                    Lesson: M03-L11
                    Expr:   b = ȳ − m · x̄
                    Use:    Completes the line equation.

[F-M04-L05-01      ]  Alpha-confidence relation                    
                    Lesson: M04-L05
                    Expr:   Confidence = 1 − α
                    Use:    Setting decision rule before testing.

[F-M04-L07-01      ]  Sample size for proportion                   
                    Lesson: M04-L07
                    Expr:   n = Z²·p·(1−p) / E²
                    Use:    Planning surveys with proportions.

[F-M04-L08-01      ]  Law of Large Numbers                         
                    Lesson: M04-L08
                    Expr:   lim (n→∞) x̄ₙ = μ
                    Use:    Justifying that bigger samples are more reliable.

[F-M04-L09-01      ]  Central Limit Theorem                        
                    Lesson: M04-L09
                    Expr:   x̄ ~ N(μ, σ²/n) for large n
                    Use:    Inference on means with large samples.

[F-M04-L09-02      ]  Standard error of mean                       
                    Lesson: M04-L09
                    Expr:   SE = σ / √n
                    Use:    Building confidence intervals.

[F-M05-L06-01      ]  Two-sample t-statistic                       
                    Lesson: M05-L06
                    Expr:   t = (x̄₁ − x̄₂) / √(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂)
                    Use:    Comparing means of two independent groups.

[F-M05-L09-01      ]  Type I error rate                            
                    Lesson: M05-L09
                    Expr:   α = P(reject H0 | H0 true)
                    Use:    Always.

[F-M05-L09-02      ]  Type II error rate                           
                    Lesson: M05-L09
                    Expr:   β = P(fail to reject H0 | H0 false)
                    Use:    Power analysis.

[F-M05-L09-03      ]  Power of a test                              
                    Lesson: M05-L09
                    Expr:   Power = 1 − β
                    Use:    Designing experiments.
